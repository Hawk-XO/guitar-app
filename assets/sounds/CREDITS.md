# Guitar sample credits

The recorded guitar notes in this folder come from the **Fluid R3 GM** SoundFont by Frank Wen,
as pre-rendered MP3s in [gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
Converted here to one MP3 per note (MIDI 40–88) — no other changes.

| Folder | Instrument |
|---|---|
| `acoustic/` | Acoustic Guitar (steel) |
| `clean/` | Electric Guitar (clean) |
| `dist/` | Distortion Guitar |
| `muted/` | Electric Guitar (muted) — used for ✕ dead notes and palm mutes |
